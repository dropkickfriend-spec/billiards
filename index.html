DEMON BILLIARDS

Bureau of Recreational Cosmology · Table Inspection Division

The oldest civilizations discovered faster-than-light travel, infinite energy, and universe manufacturing. Then they invented billiards, and immediately began causing cosmological disasters.

The balls are fundamental particles. Every shot is recorded by reality. When you aim, you see the ghost-ball futures — hundreds of faint trajectories, the cloud of everything the shot could become. The table remembers all of them, permanently.

Pot the balls. Don't create a universe. Don't create a Mandelbrot set. Don't create a demon. It's four things. You'll manage maybe one.

Or — and hear us out, goof — you could just go outside. It's right there. It's free. Nothing outside has ever become sentient because of your break shot.

Form 88-C: the Bureau is not liable for spacetime, mathematics, or life generated during recreation, and maintains it did try to get you to go outside.
